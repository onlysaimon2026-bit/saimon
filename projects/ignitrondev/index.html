[04]About IgnitronDev

We build systems,
not just software.

A small studio that takes a narrow number of engagements at a time. We work in the parts most teams postpone: the data model, the boundaries between services, the thing that pages someone at 3am.

Principle 01 Start from the problem, not a template

No reference architecture survives contact with a real domain. The shape of the system comes out of the work it has to do.

Principle 02 Make behaviour observable

A system nobody can inspect is a system nobody can operate. Logs, traces, and alerts are part of the build, not a later phase.

Principle 03 Hand it over properly

Every engagement ends with a team that can change the system without us: runbooks, decision records, and a real handover.
